Part 2 — hero & scene generator art

These use the same visual language as the lab hero images (hero-generator.html, map-game.js): the real island sprites from docs/img/islands/, the ocean gradient (#060c18 → #0e1826 → #040810), the gold hop paths (#b8862a), the node state colors (green start, purple revealed, amber target, red flag), the helicopter travel indicator, and the Capcom-style gold title. The compositions stay milestone announcements rather than lab heroes — the elements are borrowed, not the whole look.
